ProofPDF Type Editor
Aa

Open a PDF to edit its text in place. Proof reads the embedded font of every line, matches it to the closest available typeface, and lets you type directly over the original — or turn any line into a fillable form field.

Selection

Click any line of text on the page to inspect its detected font.

How it works

1
Open a PDF — every text line is read from the document's own font data.
2
Edit Text mode lets you click and retype any line; edits are drawn in the closest matching font on export.
3
Mark Form Fields mode converts selected lines into real, fillable PDF form fields.
4
Export — either a flattened edited PDF, or an interactive fillable-form PDF.
